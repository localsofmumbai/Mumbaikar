Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

Mumbaikar is a transit map app by Locals of Mumbai. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • No name, email, phone number or personal identifiers
  • No location data or GPS tracking
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No contacts or files access
  • No account or login required
  • No user-generated content

Automatically Collected Information

Standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and pages visited. This is standard practice for any website. This data is used only for diagnosing technical issues and is never used for advertising, profiling, or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The app loads map images from GitHub Pages (github.io). GitHub may collect standard server log data when images are requested. GitHub's privacy policy applies to those requests: GitHub Privacy Statement

The app is distributed via Google Play. Google's privacy policy applies to the download and installation process: Google Privacy Policy

We do not use any analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

Data Storage and Security

We do not store any personal data on our servers. The app stores only cached app files on your device for offline use (via service worker). These files contain no personal information and can be cleared by clearing your browser or app cache.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

Mumbaikar does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for all ages and requires no personal information to use. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately.

Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have any concerns, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
