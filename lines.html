Mumbai Suburban Lines

Mumbai has two major suburban railway zones — Central Railway and Western Railway — plus Metro and Monorail.

Central Line

Operated by Central Railway. Two branches:

  • Main Line — CSMT ⇌ Kasara & CSMT ⇌ Khopoli
  • Harbour Line — CSMT ⇌ Panvel & CSMT ⇌ Andheri

Trans Harbour Line

  • Thane ⇌ Vashi
  • Thane ⇌ Panvel

Western Line

Operated by Western Railway.

  • Churchgate ⇌ Dahanu Road

Metro Rail

Multiple lines operated by MMRDA across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane. See the map for all active and proposed corridors.

Monorail — Line 1

  • Chembur ⇌ Wadala Depot
